Hair Health & Recommendation Quiz

Not sure what treatment to request? Answer these four simple questions to receive a tailored styling recommendation.

1. What is your natural hair texture?

Fine & Delicate

Feels soft, breaks easily, struggles to hold volume

Medium & Balanced

Standard texture, easy to manage, holds styling well

Coarse & Thick

Feels heavy, very dense, takes long to blow dry

Wavy & Curly

Natural patterns ranging from waves to coils

2. How would you describe your hair's current health?

Healthy / Virgin

Rarely colored, soft shine, little to no split ends

Colored / Highlighted

Regular single processes, balayage, or glossing sessions

Dry & Damaged

Over-bleached, heat-damaged, prone to breakage and frizz

Natural Gray / White

Embracing natural silver tones, textured or wiry feel

3. What is your biggest daily hair struggle?

Frizz & Humidity

Expands instantly in damp air, lacks silkiness

Harsh Gray Regrowth

Flat & Lacks Volume

Lies flat to the scalp, haircuts feel heavy

Brassy / Yellowing Tones

Blondes or gray tones turning yellow over time

Low Maintenance Color

Frizz-Free & Smooth
