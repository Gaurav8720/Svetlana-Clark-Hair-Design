Behind the Chair

I notice your energy first. Your lifestyle. Then we create dimensional color, seamless gray blending, and precision cuts designed to protect long-term hair health and support the way you actually live.

Luxury Care

Pura Luxe Amino Acid Treatment

What is Pura Luxe? An advanced, non-toxic amino acid formula designed to alter the hair's internal structure. It delivers healthy, smooth, keratin-rich, frizz-free hair with beautiful reflective shine.

Unlike standard keratin formulas or Brazilian straightening options that release toxic formaldehyde fumes under high heat, Pura Luxe is completely formaldehyde-free, sulfate-free, and safe. The product is thoroughly washed from the hair fibers before flat-ironing, ensuring a zero-fume, clean-air environment during your service.

Pura Luxe can be customized to suit your curls. We can alter the timing to fully straighten curly strands, tame heavy frizz while retaining your natural patterns, or simply protect colored hair.
