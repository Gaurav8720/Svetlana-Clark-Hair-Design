Comparison

Traditional vs. Signature Styling

How does Svetlana's custom, health-first approach compare to standard high-maintenance salon treatments?

Gray Blending

vs. Solid Base Color

Traditional Dye

  • Harsh root line grows out in 3-4 weeks
  • Solid, flat pigment covers hair opacity
  • Chemical fatigue on fine cuticles
  • High maintenance: Visit every 4 weeks

Signature Blend

  • Blurred regrowth lines transition softly
  • Multi-tonal highlights mimic natural sun
  • Protective K18 peptide bond building
  • Low maintenance: Visit every 12-16 weeks

Luxury Balayage

vs. Foil Highlights

Foil Highlights

  • Brassy fade and zebra-stripe look
  • Dry texture and heavy structural stress
  • No built-in bond reconstructors
  • Requires root bleach touchups

Dimensional Balayage

  • Custom hand-painted transitions
  • Natural bounce and dimensional shimmer
  • K18 pre-treated for fine hair safety
  • Grows out beautifully for months

Pura Luxe Smoothing

vs. Keratin / Relaxers

Keratin Straightening

  • Contains toxic formaldehyde fumes
  • Leaves fine hair flat and lifeless
  • Alters permanent disulfide bonds
  • Requires avoiding moisture for days

Pura Luxe Amino

  • 100% Non-toxic and formaldehyde-free
  • Retains natural volume and health
  • Keratin-rich strengthening system
  • Wash, style, and swim immediately
